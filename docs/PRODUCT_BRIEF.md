# .json — Immersive product brief

**Product:** A versioned emotional archive accompanying the memoir *Jason, under revision*.

## Promise
The printed memoir works independently. Optional web experiences let readers explore artwork, video, voice notes and version histories tied to a chapter.

## Product layers
1. Book: canonical, complete literary narrative.
2. Free website: chapter teasers, original visual identities, archival motifs.
3. Chapter companion: artwork, audio/video, metadata and changes in narrator perspective.
4. Optional collector bundle: only after validation through paid preorders.

## Hero chapters
- Bát cơm nguội — family and legacy; existing source in repository.
- Dream journal — notebooks, yellow metro lights; rights review needed.
- Version 28.0.0 — birthday, career, friendship and gratitude.
- To Mary Jane — a tender goodbye; privacy review needed.

## Prototype scope
An accessible responsive website with four **teaser-only** chapter cards and chapter pages. No checkout, customer accounts, analytics, unapproved manuscript releases, or private media.

## Business hypotheses
Free media attracts readers; the book drives revenue; companion bundle can increase average order value; collector edition supports limited premium sales. Do not assume conversion rates without experiments.

## Validation
Interview 5–10 readers; measure whether the digital experience deepens attachment to the prose; obtain real print/fulfillment quotes; test a small preorder offer.

## Success criterion
Readers enjoy the prose first and want to return for the companion; visual effects must not substitute for literary quality.
